Electrical Power Distribution

3-Phase AC Power System Configuration

The Prophecy's reduced power requirements (1,100 MW vs Oracle's 1,375 MW) suggest a 5.5-8 kV distribution system, optimized for drone bay energy allocation and capacitor warfare modules.

Current Capacity Analysis

Given:

  • Power (P) = 1,100 MW = 1.1 × 10⁹ W
  • Voltage (V) = 6.6 kV = 6,600 V
  • Power Factor (PF) = 0.88 (drone systems + armor)

Formula: I = P / (√3 × V × PF)

I = 1.1 × 10⁹ / (1.732 × 6,600 × 0.88)
I = 1.1 × 10⁹ / 10,059.46
I ≈ 109,400 A

At 5.5 kV Distribution

I = 1.1×10⁹ / (1.732×5,500×0.88)
I ≈ 131,300 A

Higher current requires thicker conductors but simpler transformer systems

At 8 kV Distribution

I = 1.1×10⁹ / (1.732×8,000×0.88)
I ≈ 90,200 A

Lower current reduces I²R losses through ship circuits

Core Circuit Equations

⚡ Ohm's Law - Fundamental Relationships

I = E / R
E = I × R
R = E / I

I = Current (Amperes) | E = Voltage (Volts) | R = Resistance (Ohms)

⚡ Power Formulas - Energy Transfer

P = V × I (Basic Power)
P = V² / R (Voltage-based)
P = I² × R (Current-based)

P = Power (Watts)

⚡ Charge & Current Relationships

I = Q / t
Q = I × t
t = Q / I

Q = Charge (Coulombs) | t = Time (seconds)

Resistance Circuit Configurations

Series Circuits

RT = R₁ + R₂ + R₃ + ... + RN

Total resistance is the sum of all components

Voltage Divider Rule:

Vx = (Rx / RT) × E

Parallel Circuits

RT = (R₁ × R₂) / (R₁ + R₂)

For two resistors in parallel

Current Divider Rule:

I₂ = IT × (R₁ / (R₁ + R₂))

AC Power & Complex Impedance

Sinusoidal Waveforms

Current: i = Im × sin(ωt)
Voltage: e = Em × sin(ωt)
Angular Freq: ω = 2π × f

Where: Im, Em = peak amplitudes | f = frequency (Hz)

RLC Circuit Impedance

Z = R + j(XL - XC)

  • R = Resistance (real component, Ohms)
  • XL = Inductive reactance (positive imaginary)
  • XC = Capacitive reactance (negative imaginary)

Capacitive Reactance

XC = 1 / (ωC)
C = 1 / (ωXC)

Result typically in microfarads (μF)

⚠️ Tactical Configuration Notes

With 4 high slots (no turret/launcher bonuses), optimal fitting prioritizes:
